How it works

From doorstep to resolution, in the open

01 — Lodge

Describe the issue

Tell us what's wrong, where, and add a photo if you have one. Takes under two minutes.

02 — Acknowledge

Ward office reviews

Your ward office reviews the complaint and confirms it's been received.

03 — Act

Field team responds

A field officer is assigned and works the issue — you can track progress by your complaint ID.

04 — Resolve

Verified & closed

The officer records what was done and when. Nothing is marked resolved without a named signature.
